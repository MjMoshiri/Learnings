# Learning Swift

Working through SwiftUI and the Apple ecosystem. Started with state management (the `@State` / `@Binding` / `@ObservedObject` family) and basic interface styling.

Coming from Kotlin and Compose, SwiftUI's declarative model felt familiar. The differences are in the lifecycle and in how Apple expects you to think about app structure (App, Scene, View). Notes for both areas live under `notes/ios-swift/`.
