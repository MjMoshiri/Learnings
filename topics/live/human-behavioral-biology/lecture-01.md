# The gist

- We bucket behavior into clean labels — genetic, cultural, hormonal — and the buckets give wrong answers.
- Two ideas in the same bucket look more alike than they are. Two in different buckets look more different. Everything at the boundary gets lost.
- Behavior has to be explained at every time scale at once: seconds (nervous system), minutes to hours (sensory, hormones), days to months (plasticity), years (childhood, fetal environment), conception (genes), centuries (culture, ecology), millions of years (evolution).

## Makes you think

Each level on that time-scale list explains a piece of the behavior. None of them is the whole story. None is enough on its own. You don't get to skip any either — you need all of them to actually understand why someone did what they did. The question is never which bucket; it's how the layers feed each other.
