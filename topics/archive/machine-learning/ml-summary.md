# ML Summary (40-day sprint)

What I covered in roughly 40 days of focused ML study.

## Models
Worked through the standard families: linear and logistic regression, decision trees, random forests, gradient boosting (XGBoost, CatBoost), SVMs, k-NN, naive Bayes, basic neural nets.

## Mathematics
The math that actually shows up: linear algebra (matrix decompositions, eigenvalues, SVD), probability and statistics, multivariable calculus for gradients, convex optimization basics. Worked through the relevant chapters of *Mathematics for Machine Learning*.

## Training and optimization
Loss functions, gradient descent variants, regularization (L1/L2/dropout), learning rate schedules, hyperparameter search, cross-validation.

## EDA
Pandas-based exploration: distributions, missing data, correlations, target-variable behavior. Standard plots before any modeling.

## Data viz
Matplotlib and seaborn for static plots. Just enough to read the data, not to make publication figures.

## Data cleaning
Missing-value handling, outlier detection, deduplication, type coercion. The unglamorous half.

## Feature engineering
Encoding (one-hot, target, ordinal), scaling, binning, interactions, feature selection, dimensionality reduction (PCA).

## Libraries
`pandas`, `numpy`, `matplotlib`, `seaborn`, `scikit-learn`, `statsmodels`, `xgboost`, `catboost`, `tensorflow`, `keras`.
